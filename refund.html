Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026. This is our merchant refund policy for digital goods. Wayl also maintains its own refund and dispute process for payments made through its checkout.

Folio Studio sells digital files (PDF guides). There is no physical shipment to cancel in transit. Please read a product page before you buy, including the format, the launch price, and what the guide contains.

How to request a refund

Write to [contact email] from the email address used at checkout. Include the title you bought, the approximate date of purchase, and the reason for the request.

When we will refund

We will refund you if:

  • you have not received the file, and we cannot deliver it after you have contacted us;
  • the file is damaged or will not open, and we cannot provide a working copy;
  • the product you received is materially different from the description on the product page;
  • you were charged in error, or charged twice for the same title.

You may also request a refund within 14 days of purchase if the guide was not useful for the purpose described on its product page, provided you tell us why. We review these requests in good faith. This 14-day window is our own policy for these digital goods.

When we may decline

We may decline a refund if we reasonably believe the request is abusive (for example, repeated purchases and refunds of the same title), or if it is made after the 14-day window and the file was delivered as described. We will explain our decision in writing.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Wayl. Wayl’s published refund guidance states that refund requests to Wayl should be made within 30 days of the transaction, that approved refunds are processed in 5–10 business days, and that money is sent back to the original payment method. The time it takes for the funds to appear can also depend on your bank or card issuer.

Cancellations

If you have paid but not yet received a file, contact us immediately and we will treat that as a cancellation and refund. Once a working file has been delivered, the refund rules above apply instead of a simple cancellation.

Payment disputes

If you dispute a charge through your bank or card issuer, Wayl will notify us. We will keep order records so that we can respond. We prefer that you contact us first so we can try to fix a delivery problem or issue a refund without a formal dispute.
