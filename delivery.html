Delivery Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. Folio Studio delivers digital products only.

What is delivered

Each purchase is a digital product, in the format stated on the product page. There is no physical parcel, courier, or shipping charge.

When and how you receive it

After payment is confirmed at checkout, we send a download link to the email address used at checkout. Delivery should follow shortly after confirmation, typically within a few minutes. You should keep a copy of the file for your own records.

If the email does not arrive

Please wait two hours and check your spam or promotions folder. If the link is still missing, write to [contact email] from the same email address and include the title you bought. We will resend the file or the link.

Failed or incomplete payment

We do not send a file until payment is confirmed. If a payment is declined or left unfinished, no download is due.

Technical requirements

You need an email inbox you can access and a device that can open the file format stated on the product page. If a file will not open, contact us and we will help you obtain a working copy, as described in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Access period

Download links may expire after a reasonable period. [link expiry period, once the delivery method is set]. If a link expires, contact us and we will provide a new one to the original buyer.
