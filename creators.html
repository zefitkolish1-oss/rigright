For creators

You already have the content. We help it earn.

If you teach a subject in public — on video, in a newsletter, or anywhere your audience already finds you — we can help turn that material into a paid digital product. You remain the author. We remain the studio behind the edition.

The offer, in plain terms

You, the creator

40%

of sales revenue. No upfront fee.

Folio Studio

60%

for writing, design, store, payments, and delivery.

  • No upfront cost. If we accept the work, we are paid from the studio’s share of sales, as set out in a written agreement before we begin.
  • You keep ownership. You keep the copyright in your underlying material. We need a licence to edit, design, and sell the published edition. That licence is described in the agreement.
  • Approval rights. The product does not go on sale until you have approved the text and the presentation. Substantive changes after approval are agreed in writing.
  • We handle the rest. Writing and shaping, design, the sales page, secure checkout, and digital delivery. You may also promote the product to your own audience with a unique link.

We look for creators with about 1,000 to 10,000 followers, or more, in any niche. A clear subject and material we can stand behind matter more than a huge following. We are selective, and we will say no if the work is not a fit.

Our first published line is RigRight PC Guides. It is an example of the finished work, not a promise that every partnership will look the same.
