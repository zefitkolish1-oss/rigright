For creators

We publish with you. We do not buy you out.

If you already teach a subject in videos, newsletters, or notes, we can help turn that material into a paid guide. You remain the author. We remain the publisher of that edition.

What we offer

  • Revenue share. Sales income is split according to a written agreement. The exact percentage is agreed before work starts and stated in the contract. We do not quote a single rate on this page because it depends on the work involved.
  • You keep ownership. You keep the copyright in your underlying material. We need a licence to edit, design, and sell the published edition. That licence is described in the agreement, not buried in marketing copy.
  • Approval rights. The guide does not go on sale until you have approved the text and the cover. Substantive changes after approval are agreed in writing.
  • Production and sales. We handle editing, design, the sales page, payment, and digital delivery. You may also promote the guide to your own audience with a unique link.

We are selective. A clear subject, a real audience, and material we can stand behind matter more than follower counts.
