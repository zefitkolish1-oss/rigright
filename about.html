About

A small studio that helps creators earn from what they already know.

Abdulrazzaq

Tellvora Studio is a creator partnership studio. We work with people who already teach a subject in public, and we turn that knowledge into a premium digital product — a guide, a course, a summary, or a set of templates — that can earn without the creator having to be on camera for every sale.

Abdulrazzaq started the studio to do this work carefully: fewer projects, a written agreement, and a finished product he is willing to put a name to.

This website is for creator partnerships only. It is not a shop. We do not publish sales counts or testimonials we cannot verify.
