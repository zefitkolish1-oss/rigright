About

A studio for useful books, not content mills.

Folio Studio is a digital publishing studio. The name is a placeholder until the official studio name is confirmed.

We work with creators who already know a subject well. Our job is to turn that knowledge into a guide someone can buy, read, and use: structured, edited, and designed with the same care we would want as readers.

The first product line is RigRight — The Ultimate PC Guide, a set of buying and building guides for personal computers. Later lines will cover other practical subjects as we take on new authors.

We sell digital files only. There is no print run and no physical shipment. Payment is processed by Wayl. We do not store card numbers on this website.
