FAQ

Questions we expect to be asked.

What does Folio Studio do?

We partner with creators to turn the content they already have into a premium digital product — a guide or a course. We handle the writing and shaping, the design, the store, secure checkout, and delivery. The creator keeps ownership of the underlying work and takes a share of sales.

How is the money split?

The standard split is 40% of sales revenue to the creator and 60% to Folio Studio. There is no upfront fee. Our share covers writing, design, the store, payments, and delivery. The same figures are written into the agreement before work starts.

Who can apply?

Creators with about 1,000 to 10,000 followers, or more, in any niche. A clear subject and material we can stand behind matter more than a huge following. We are selective, and applying does not mean we will take the work on. The For Creators page has the application form.

Do you take my rights?

No. You keep ownership of your underlying work. We need a licence to publish the edition, and you approve the text and presentation before sale. Details are on the For Creators page and in the written agreement.

What is RigRight?

RigRight PC Guides is our first product line: practical computer-buying and building products, sold under their own name in the shop. It is an example of our work, not a second company name for the studio. Folio Studio is the studio; RigRight is the product line.

What do I receive when I buy from the shop?

A downloadable digital product, delivered as a link after payment is confirmed. Product pages state the format. The Gaming Builds title is 23 pages and has a free sample on the shop page. Page counts for the other titles are still being finalised and are marked as [page count]. See the Delivery Policy.

How quickly will I get my purchase?

Delivery is digital and should be prompt after payment is confirmed, usually within a few minutes. If you have not received the email within two hours, check your spam folder, then write to [contact email].

What are the launch prices in the RigRight shop?

Prices are in US dollars. The first figure is the regular price; the second is the current launch price.

  • Office & Research: $12, launch price $9 (−25%)
  • Tablets & Touch Laptops: $12, launch price $9 (−25%)
  • Design & Creative: $15, launch price $12 (−20%)
  • Gaming Builds: $19, launch price $15 (−21%). A free sample is on the shop page.
  • AI Workstations: $24, launch price $19 (−21%), sold separately from the bundle
  • Complete Bundle — 4 Guides (Office, Tablets, Design, and Gaming; AI sold separately): $39, launch price $25 (save 36%)

Launch prices are temporary. When the launch period ends, the regular prices apply. See the shop.

Is the AI Workstations title in the bundle?

No. The bundle includes Office & Research, Tablets & Touch Laptops, Design & Creative, and Gaming Builds. AI Workstations is sold only as a separate title.

How do I pay?

Purchases go through a secure checkout on a payment page run by our payment provider. We do not take card details on this website. Card and payment-method availability is determined by the provider. Payment links will be added to the shop when they are ready.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in the cases described in our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Because these are digital products, we also describe the limits of that policy clearly on that page.
