FAQ

Questions we expect to be asked.

What does Tellvora Studio do?

We partner with creators to turn the content they already have into a premium digital product — a guide, a course, a summary, or a set of templates. We handle the writing and shaping, the design, the store, payments, and delivery. The creator keeps ownership of the underlying work and takes a share of sales, under a written agreement.

How does the 40/60 split work?

The standard offer is that the creator receives 40% of sales revenue and Tellvora Studio receives 60%. The studio share covers writing, design, the store, payments, and delivery. There is no upfront fee. The same percentages are stated again in the written agreement before work begins.

Is there an upfront fee?

No. If we accept the work, the studio is paid from its share of sales, as set out in the agreement. We may decline a project if it is not a fit.

Who do you work with?

Creators who already publish — on video, in a newsletter, or in any other public channel — and who have something practical to teach. We look for people with about 1,000 to 10,000 followers, or more, in any niche. A clear subject and material we can stand behind matter more than a huge following. We are selective.

Do I keep the rights to my work?

Yes. You keep the copyright in your underlying material. We need a licence to edit, design, and sell the published edition. That licence is described in the written agreement. The product does not go out under the partnership until you have approved the text and the presentation.

What can you build from my content?

Depending on the material you already have, we can shape a guide, a course, a concise summary, or templates such as worksheets and checklists. We agree the format in writing before work begins. This is a description of formats, not a catalogue of items for sale on this website.

Does this website sell anything?

No. This website is for creator partnerships only. It is not a shop, and it does not take payments.

How do I apply?

Use the form on the For Creators page, write to us from the Contact page, or email tellvorastudio@gmail.com.

What if you decide not to take the project on?

We will say no if the work is not a fit. We do not start production, and no agreement is formed, until both sides have signed a written contract.
