FAQ

Questions we expect to be asked.

What do I receive when I buy a guide?

A PDF file, delivered as a download link after payment is confirmed. The Gaming Builds guide is 23 pages. Page counts for the other titles are still being finalised and are marked as [page count] on the shop page. See the Delivery Policy.

How quickly will I get the file?

Delivery is digital and should be prompt after Wayl confirms payment, usually within a few minutes. If you have not received the email within two hours, check your spam folder, then write to [contact email].

Why are there two prices on each title?

The lower price is a launch price. The struck-through figure is the regular price we intend to use after the launch period. When the launch period ends, the regular price will apply. We will not present a launch price as a permanent discount.

What are the launch prices?

Prices are in US dollars. The first figure is the regular price; the second is the current launch price.

  • Office & Research: $12, launch price $9 (−25%)
  • Tablets & Touch Laptops: $12, launch price $9 (−25%)
  • Design & Creative: $15, launch price $12 (−20%)
  • Gaming Builds: $19, launch price $15 (−21%). A free sample PDF is on the shop page.
  • AI Workstations: $24, launch price $19 (−21%), sold separately from the bundle
  • Complete Bundle — 4 Guides (Office, Tablets, Design, and Gaming; AI sold separately): $39, launch price $25 (save 36%)

Launch prices are temporary. When the launch period ends, the regular prices apply. See the shop.

Is the AI Workstations guide in the bundle?

No. The bundle includes Office & Research, Tablets & Touch Laptops, Design & Creative, and Gaming Builds. AI Workstations is sold only as a separate title.

Who is RigRight for?

People choosing or building a personal computer and who want a clear written reference, not a live consultancy. The guides are written in English and priced in US dollars on this site. They are not a substitute for a technician if you are unsure about opening a case or handling power supplies.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled by Wayl. You will be sent to a Wayl payment page to complete the purchase. Card and payment-method availability is determined by Wayl. We do not take card details on this website. The footer of every page states that payments are processed securely by Wayl.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in the cases described in our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Because these are digital files, we also describe the limits of that policy clearly on that page.

I am a creator. Do you take my rights?

No. You keep ownership of your underlying work. We need a licence to publish the edition, and you approve the text and cover before sale. Details are on the For Creators page and in the written agreement.
