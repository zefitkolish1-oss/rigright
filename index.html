What we do

From notes and videos to a finished guide.

Many creators already know their subject. What they often lack is the time to structure it, fact-check it, typeset it, and sell it without guesswork. That is the work we take on.

Our first line is RigRight — The Ultimate PC Guide: a set of practical computer-buying and building guides, sold individually or as a bundle. More lines will follow as we take on new subjects.

How a partnership works

  1. 1
    We review the material. You send the draft, outline, or existing content. We say plainly whether it can become a guide.
  2. 2
    We edit and design together. You keep ownership and approve the text and cover before anything goes on sale.
  3. 3
    We sell and deliver. Buyers get an instant digital download. Revenue is split as agreed in writing. You may also share your own audience with a unique link.
