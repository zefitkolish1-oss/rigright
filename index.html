How it works

From the content you already have, to a product that can sell without you being on camera.

Many creators already know their subject. What they often lack is the time to shape it, design it, and sell it without guesswork. That is the work we take on.

  1. We review what you already have. You send videos, notes, an outline, or a draft. We say plainly whether it can become a product.
  2. We write, edit, and design with you. You keep ownership of your underlying work, and you approve the text and the presentation before anything goes on sale.
  3. We run the store. We handle the sales page, secure checkout, and delivery. Buyers receive their purchase digitally after payment is confirmed.
  4. You earn a share as it sells. You receive 40% of the sales revenue, including orders that come in when you are not online. You may also share the product with your own audience through a unique link.

Who it is for

Creators with a real audience, in any niche.

We work with people who already publish — on video, in newsletters, or in any other public channel — and who have something practical to teach.

  • About 1,000 to 10,000 followers, or more. A clear subject and material we can stand behind matter more than a huge following. We are selective.
  • Any niche, so long as we can turn the material into a product a buyer can use, and so long as we are willing to put our name next to it.
  • No upfront fee. If we take a project on, our compensation is the studio’s share of sales, as agreed in writing.

The split

You take 40%. We take 60%, and we do the production work.
